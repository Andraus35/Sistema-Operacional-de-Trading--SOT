Contexto secundario (WebSearch 2026-10-04, DADO):
"The drawback of asynchronous communication through a message broker is increased complexity,
introducing a message broker as another infrastructure component you have to manage."
"For a true single-server monolith without distributed requirements, a simple outbox polling
pattern may provide reliability without the operational overhead of maintaining a separate
message broker system."
