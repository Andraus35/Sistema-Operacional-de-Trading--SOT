"HMMs model the underlying market dynamics as a system that transitions between
different states, each with its own characteristic behavior patterns in terms of
returns, volatility, and other market metrics."

"Financial markets typically exhibit several characteristic regimes:" (Trending
Markets, Volatile Markets, Range-bound Markets).

"Trading systems can use HMM-based regime detection to: Dynamically adjust risk
controls based on the current regime [and] Switch between different trading
strategies optimized for specific regimes."
