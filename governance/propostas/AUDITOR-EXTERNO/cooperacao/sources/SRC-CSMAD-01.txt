"To make contradictory statement generation reliable, we add a Natural Language Inference (NLI) based verifier that checks whether the generated statement actually contradicts the original claim. CSMAD asks independent agents to evaluate the claim and the contradictory claim, which encourages different lines of reasoning without assigning preset stances."
